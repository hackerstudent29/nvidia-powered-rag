## 💡 Key Facts & Admission Guidelines

- **TNEA Code:** 1301 (for B.E. / B.Tech admissions via Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions counselling)
- **Affiliation:** Anna University, Chennai | **Approval:** AICTE, New Delhi
- **Location:** SIPCOT IT Park, Siruseri, Chennai – 603 103

- **NRI Quota:** 5% of approved intake available under NRI category
