## 3. MTC Public Bus Routes & Siruseri IT Park Connectors

For students and staff opting for public Metropolitan Transport Corporation (MTC) transit, the following direct MTC buses connect major Chennai hubs to MSAJCEA Campus (Siruseri IT Park / SIPCOT Gate):

| MTC Bus Route | Origin ↔ Destination | Key Intermediate Stops Serving MSAJCEA | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MTC 515** | Tambaram ↔ Kelambakkam | Tambaram, Vandalur Zoo, Mambakkam, Kelambakkam (Change for Siruseri / Campus) | Every 15 mins |
| **MTC 555 / 555S** | Tambaram / Kilambakkam (KCBT) ↔ Siruseri IT Park / Sholinganallur | Tambaram, Vandalur, Kelambakkam, Siruseri IT Park (MSAJCEA Gate) | Every 15–20 mins |
| **MTC 570 / AC-570** | CMBT (Koyambedu) ↔ Kelambakkam | Vadapalani, Ashok Pillar, Guindy, Velachery, Sholinganallur, Siruseri IT Park (MSAJCEA Gate) | Every 5–10 mins |
| **MTC 102 / 102X** | Broadway (Central) ↔ Kelambakkam | Broadway, Mylapore, Adyar, Perungudi, Sholinganallur, Siruseri IT Park (MSAJCEA Gate) | Every 10 mins |
| **MTC 19K** | Adyar O.T. ↔ Siruseri IT Park | Adyar, Thiruvanmiyur, Kandanchavadi, Perungudi, Sholinganallur, Siruseri IT Park | Every 20 mins |
| **MTC 568B** | Velachery ↔ Thiruporur | Velachery Vijayanagar, Sholinganallur, Siruseri IT Park, Kelambakkam | Every 30 mins |

> 📌 **Note**: All MTC buses stopping at Siruseri IT Park or SIPCOT Main Gate drop passengers within 2–3 minutes of MSAJCEA main entrance. Frequent share-autos and campus shuttle mini-vans operate continuously from Siruseri SIPCOT entrance to the engineering college building.
