# Vision, Mission, and Quality Policy

## 1. Institutional Vision
- **Vision**: To be an eminent institute for higher education and research through innovative teaching-learning and sustainable practices to meet industrial and societal needs.

## 2. Institutional Mission
The mission of **MSAJCEA** comprises three foundational pillars:
- **M1 (Quality Technical Education)**: Imparting quality technical education by transforming students into professionally competent technocrats.
- **M2 (Sustainable Development & Career)**: Nurturing students in all-round sustainable development for their successful careers and self-employability.
- **M3 (Ethics & Civic Responsibilities)**: Inculcating critical thinking and professional ethics alongside civic responsibilities by instilling strong core values.

## 3. Quality Policy
- **Quality Commitment**: **MSAJCEA** is committed to creating quality professionals through innovative teaching, research, and industrial relations.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Focusing on nurturing human values and ethics to meet industrial and societal needs on a continual improvement basis.
